<div class="section-head"><div class="section-num">THE ZONING REFORM</div><h2 class="section-title">What happened in Arlington</h2></div>
<div class="card-grid">
<article class="card clay">
<p>"Expanded Housing Options" (up to 6 units/lot) faced a legal challenge that paused missing-middle permits for roughly a year; a Court of Appeals decision reinstated the policy in June 2025, with the case potentially still headed to the state Supreme Court.</p>

<div class="section-head"><div class="section-num">GENERAL PLAN LANGUAGE</div><h2 class="section-title">In the plan's own words</h2></div>
<div class="card-grid">
<article class="card clay">
<div class="quote-stack">
<p class="quote"><b>Vision</b>"Arlington will be a diverse and inclusive world-class urban community with secure, attractive residential and commercial neighborhoods..."</p>
<p class="quote"><b>Corridors/centers goal</b>"Concentrate high-density development within Metro Station Areas in the Rosslyn-Ballston and Route 1/Richmond Highway Metrorail Transit Corridors."</p>
<p class="quote"><b>EHO/GLUP amendment, Section 5.4 (2023)</b>Adopted the <em>same day</em> as the zoning ordinance changes, "adds language to the GLUP map to include accessory dwelling units, and EHO uses in areas planned for 'Low' Residential uses."</p>
</div>
<div class="note"><b>Why this one matters most:</b> unlike Gainesville, Arlington's "Expanded Housing Options" was <em>not</em> a zoning-only change — the County amended both the GLUP and the Zoning Ordinance the same day. A 2024 circuit court raised narrow <em>procedural</em> questions unrelated to comp-plan consistency (an infrastructure study, a public notice, a delegation-of-authority detail), and the policy was reinstated on appeal in June 2025. The takeaway: a comp-plan anchor and a carefully documented adoption <em>process</em> reinforce each other.</div>
<p class="borrow"><b>Borrow:</b> amend the General Plan and the Land Development Code together (as Arlington did) — but also paper-trail every procedural step (impact studies, public notice language, delegation of authority) as carefully as the policy substance.</p>
