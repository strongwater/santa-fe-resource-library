<div class="section-head"><div class="section-num">THE UPDATE UNDERWAY</div><h2 class="section-title">What Santa Fe Forward covers</h2></div>
<div class="card-grid">
<article class="card">
<p>Santa Fe's current General Plan dates to 1999. <a href="https://santafeforward.org/learn-more">Santa Fe Forward</a> is the city's effort to replace it, paired with a rewrite of the Land Development Code rather than sequencing the two updates years apart — the same pairing this library's research points to as the more durable order of operations (see <a href="/research/general-plan-reforms/#pcs-gainesville">Gainesville</a>, <a href="/research/general-plan-reforms/#pcs-arlington">Arlington</a>, and <a href="/research/general-plan-reforms/#pcs-alexandria">Alexandria</a>'s experiences in Effective General Plan Reforms).</p>
<p class="muted">This library was built to feed that process with outside evidence: what other cities' plans actually say, what reforms held up over time and which didn't, what incentive programs made building easier rather than more restricted, what a modern land development code looks like, and how other cities have balanced historic preservation with housing need.</p>
