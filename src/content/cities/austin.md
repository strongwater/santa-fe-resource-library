<div class="section-head"><div class="section-num">THE ZONING REFORM</div><h2 class="section-title">What Austin did</h2></div>
<div class="card-grid">
<article class="card">
<p>Allows up to three units on most single-family lots; Phase 2 cut minimum lot sizes (to ~1,800 sq ft in many areas) so small-lot infill actually pencils out financially, rather than only rezoning to multifamily.</p>

<div class="section-head"><div class="section-num">AFFORDABLE HOUSING INCENTIVE</div><h2 class="section-title">S.M.A.R.T. Housing &amp; Affordability Unlocked</h2></div>
<div class="card-grid">
<article class="card">
<p>Beyond the HOME zoning changes above, Austin runs two incentive programs on top: S.M.A.R.T. Housing waives permit and inspection fees for income-restricted, accessible, transit-near developments, and Affordability Unlocked (2019) waives height, parking, and setback standards outright for projects where at least half the units are income-restricted — no rezoning or hearing needed.</p>
<div class="stat-row">
<span class="stat"><b>~46,000</b> total units under Austin's density-bonus programs to date</span>
<span class="stat"><b>~13,000</b> of those affordable</span>
</div>
<p class="borrow"><b>Borrow:</b> pair a zoning change (HOME) with an opt-in incentive that waives standards outright rather than granting only a small bonus — the two reinforce each other.</p>

<section class="sub">
<div class="section-head"><div class="section-num">GENERAL PLAN LANGUAGE</div><h2 class="section-title">In the plan's own words</h2></div>
<div class="card-grid">
<article class="card">
<div class="quote-stack">
<p class="quote"><b>Vision</b>"As it approaches its 200th anniversary, Austin is a beacon of sustainability, social equality, and economic opportunity... where the necessities of life are affordable and accessible to all."</p>
<p class="quote"><b>Core concept</b>"The core concepts of Imagine Austin – complete communities and compact, connected centers – are two sides of the same coin."</p>
<p class="quote"><b>Growth Concept Map</b>"The Growth Concept Map directs new and redevelopment to focused centers of varying sizes."</p>
<p class="quote"><b>Named centers</b>48 specific, geocoded centers typed as Regional/Town/Neighborhood/Job Centers — e.g. "Mueller Station Town Center," "Plaza Saltillo Neighborhood Center."</p>
<p class="quote"><b>Legal teeth</b>"Future zoning decisions may not disregard the comprehensive plan and must be made in accordance with the comprehensive plan."</p>
</div>
<p>The vision is translated into a literal map of 48 named, located centers linked by corridors chosen against explicit, checkable criteria — plus eight named "Priority Programs" that are the actual work programs (one became the CodeNEXT/land-code rewrite).</p>
<p class="borrow"><b>Borrow:</b> a single growth-concept map that names and geographically fixes each center/corridor; explicit criteria for what counts as a "corridor"; a short numbered list of priority implementation programs.</p>
